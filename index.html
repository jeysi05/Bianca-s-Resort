01 The story

An hour from Manila. Ten degrees cooler. Water that comes out of the ground warm.

Pansol has been a hot spring destination for generations — the water here runs warm from the mountain and stays warm through the night. Bianca's is a two-villa property on that stretch, built for families and groups who want the entire place to themselves.

Each villa sleeps twenty-four across four air-conditioned rooms. Two pools — one for swimming, one for kids with an optional jacuzzi. A covered pavilion with a billiard table, karaoke, arcade, and a full kitchen. You don't share it with anyone.

The best way to know if it's right for your group is to see it. Every photo on this page is the actual resort — same gate, same pools, same rooms. No stock. No staging.

03 What's included

Everything you'd expect. Nothing shared.

Every booking covers the entire villa — both pools, all four rooms, the pavilion, the kitchen, and every game. No corkage for the space itself, no strangers in your pool.

i.

Natural lukewarm spring water

Genuine water from the Pansol spring, feeding both pools. Warm at night, warm in the morning.

ii.

Four air-conditioned rooms

Three double beds per room, sleeps six. One room on the ground floor for seniors. Each room has its own toilet, bath, and TV.

iii.

Two pools, one jacuzzi

Adult pool at 4–5 feet with a basketball ring. Kiddie pool at 3 feet. Optional jacuzzi is ₱500 per hour.

iv.

Full kitchen

Two-burner gas stove with free gasul, frying pans and pots, microwave, refrigerator, toaster, water dispenser, and a BBQ grill outside. Bring your own charcoal.

v.

Covered pavilion

Eight-seater dining table, lifetime tables and chairs, karaoke, billiard table, arcade machine, and free WiFi — all under one roof.

vi.

Parking

Two gated parking slots inside the compound, plus two outside. Two parking slots per villa.
