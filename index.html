01 The story

An hour from Manila. Ten degrees cooler. Water that comes out of the ground warm.

Pansol has been a hot spring destination for generations — the water here runs warm from the mountain and stays warm through the night. Bianca's is a single property on that stretch, built for families and groups who want the entire place to themselves.

Four air-conditioned rooms sleep twenty-four. Two pools — one for swimming, one for kids with an optional jacuzzi. A covered pavilion with a billiard table, pingpong, darts, unlimited videoke, and a full kitchen. You don't share it with anyone.

The best way to know if it's right for your group is to see it. Every photo on this page is the actual resort — same gate, same pools, same rooms. No stock. No staging.

03 What's included

Everything you'd expect. Nothing shared.

Every booking covers the entire property — both pools, all four rooms, the pavilion, the kitchen, and every game. No corkage for the space itself, no strangers in your pool.

i.

Natural spring water

Genuine lukewarm water from the Pansol spring, feeding both pools. Warm at night, warm in the morning.

ii.

Four air-conditioned rooms

Each with its own toilet, bath, and TV. Enough for twenty-four guests to sleep in real beds, not on floors.

iii.

Two pools, one jacuzzi

An adult pool for swimming and a kiddie pool for the little ones. Optional jacuzzi runs ₱500 per hour.
